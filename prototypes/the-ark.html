One Family's Journey

In 2019, Noah faced one of the biggest battles of his life: a bone marrow transplant.

Long hospital days.
Uncertainty.
Fear.

Yet amid the hardship, his family experienced kindness, support, and hope from people who walked alongside them.

Those moments would later inspire something much bigger.

A Promise Born From Loss

But not every family had the same support.

Two Filipino families lost their children while fighting cancer.

Their stories became a promise:

No family should have to face illness alone.

The Moment Everything Changed

What if families didn't have to choose between continuing treatment and basic necessities?

What if support, shelter, meals, and care were available to every child fighting illness?

That question became Little Ark Foundation.

Little Ark Was Born

In 2021, Little Ark Foundation began as a place of refuge, hope, and holistic support for children and families facing critical illnesses.

Why Little Ark?

An ark is a place of refuge.

A place of safety.

A place where hope survives storms.

Little Ark was built on that same belief:

That no child and no family should face illness alone.

The Promise

We promise to walk alongside families.

Through fear.
Through uncertainty.
Through treatment.
Through hope.

“No child should have to stop treatment because their family lacks the resources to continue.”

What began as one family’s story became hope for many.

And with your support,
the story continues.
